About Ping Foundation

More Than Just a Platform.

Yeng is the brainchild of the Ping Foundation. We leverage technology to better enhance your alma mater experience through participation from fellow students, faculties and the general public.

We believe in open source, free training, and the power of networking to build empty spaces where true innovation can happen.

100% Open Source

Transparent, community-driven development.

For Everyone

Not just students, but a holistic ecosystem.

Our Mission & Vision

The Mission

To streamline university life and empower the youth towards excellence by providing an open and innovative digital platform that bridges the gap between campus life and modern technology.

The Vision

A world where academic institutions are deeply interconnected, driven by open-source collaboration, free networking, and bleeding-edge technological ecosystems.

Our Key Modules

Discover the diverse features that make Yeng a comprehensive university platform.

P2P Chatting

Seamless peer-to-peer communication tailored for student collaboration and academic discussions.

Live Sessions

Integrated WebRTC for scalable and efficient virtual classes and live streaming events.

Event Notifications

Real-time alerts for university events, keeping everyone updated and engaged instantly.

Real-time Results

Fast, accessible real-time delivery of university results directly to your mobile device.

Public Interaction

Bridging the gap between the university and the broader community with dedicated forums.

Modern Tech Stack

Powered by Node.js, MongoDB, Digital-Ocean, Nginx Load Balancing, and rich native Android UI.
